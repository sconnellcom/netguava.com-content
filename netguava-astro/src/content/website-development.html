Our Seven-Step Process

Our proven 7 phase website design and development process will ensure quality results that best represent your company.

1 Analysis

During the first step of your web development package, we take an analysis of your needs, your budget and your business model. The first stage of web design is a full understanding of what you're looking for.

We make sure to ask the right questions to get every piece of information needed for a successful website. What is your website's purpose? What are the website's goals? Who is your primary audience?

3 Design

For step 3 of your web development, we start to take a look at the raw, technical aspects of your site's design. In order to fill in your website's content and structure, step three establishes a structure and skeleton to fill in.

This web design stage includes a "wireframe" model of the site - a tool to make sure everything has a place. We put special emphasis on ensuring that we nail down the exact visual style of your site. We do this by blending your preferences with our expertise to create a stunning, industry-catered website appearance.

5 Development

During the development stage of your web design package, we'll take an in-depth look at your site's performance ability. This means optimizing your website's HTML and CSS design. We'll also ensure proper databases are in place to store and maintain your site's content and client-generating abilities for as long as you need.

The development stage of your web design package also takes a heavy look at responsiveness and load times. A responsive, quick-to-load web page is guaranteed to see more traffic and conversions than an unoptimized alternative. We make absolutely sure that your site is performing at 100%, every time someone opens your link.

7 Deployment

The final step of your web design, deployment is when your new website goes live. The first few weeks are almost always the most stressful - step 7 makes sure that your site has the manpower and knowledge behind it to succeed. Step 7 is when we fix any bugs that slipped through the cracks, and monitor the site to ensure its continued performance.

2 Planning

During step 2 of your web design experience, we'll take you through our planning process as it aligns with your goals. Then, we build a site frame that's ideal and suited for your niche and customers.

This stage of web development includes a sitemap, a structure outline, and the technological focus. We use techniques drawn from your industry and your niche to build an online experience that's right for you.

Utilizing these tools, we'll build an outline to integrate your services seamlessly intodesign-development-step the digital world.

4 Content

By the time step 4 finishes, your web development's content design and copy writing will be complete. Step 4 of your web design plan is all about building the content for your website that you want to see. We utilize industry research to create a fully optimized, consumer-ready content for your future visitors to enjoy.

This step doesn't just stop at building content that's interesting to read. Our content designers take a top-down approach to ensure that your content is search-engine optimized. Search engine optimization has been linked to a better advertisement performance, higher conversions, and better results for the top search engines.

6 Testing

As the sixth stage and final change area before deployment, step 6 is designed to do one thing: test. The last stage of web design has us stress-testing your website in every area possible. We make sure it performs under any load or pressure and no matter how unusual the request.

That means ensuring that all of your website's technical aspects are working exactly the way they're intended to. It means ensuring that your website works for any browser, on any device. And finally, we take a comprehensive look back at the process, and ask you, and ourselves: "does this site serve its purpose? Does it do what it's supposed to?"
