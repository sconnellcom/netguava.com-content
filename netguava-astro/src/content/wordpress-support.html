Why Is Maintenance So Important?Expand

When a vulnerability is discovered in WordPress, a new version is released to address the issue. Once a new release is available, websites running out of date versions of WordPress become even more vulnerable to attack because the information required to exploit the vulnerability is available to the public within the update. WordPress releases one to two major updates or security updates every two months. For this reason it is important that a WordPress website is always kept up to date.
