The Details

Analytics & Tracking

Analytics is a key tool used to determine how your site is being used and how we can make it more effective. We create custom reports from your visitor tracking data to help you better understand how your site is serving your visitors.

Marketing & Advertising

Having a targeted pay-per-click ad campaign drives more traffic and enables more visitor tracking. This data is used to optimize website and marketing effectiveness so we can drive your sales.

Website Performance

Speed is not only important to user experience, Google has indicated site speed is one of the signals used by its algorithm to rank pages. Pages with a longer load time tend to have higher bounce rates and lower average time on page. Longer load times have been shown to negatively affect sales and conversions.

Web Hosting

Let us ensure your site is always online and loads fast every time. We specialize in fast and secure WordPress hosting and are obsessive about our off-site backups. Don't let an inexpensive hosting plan turn your customers away.

Sales, E-Commerce & Leads

A strong call to action will encourage visitors to connect with you through well-designed and strategically placed lead capture forms.

We know what it takes to run a successful online store and have the development experience needed to customize your online store to your business needs.

Online Business Listings

Google and Bing Places for Business get you in front of your customers. We make sure you'll stand out, when people are looking for you on Google or Bing Search and Maps.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Let us give your website a foundation that search engines will favor and visitors will love. Our online presence audit, analysis, and optimization is the corner stone of your targeted SEO campaign.

Social Media

Social media is an invaluable tool for cost-effective marketing, lead capture, and SEO. Social presence, accurate company information as well as links to and from your website are key.

Web Support

Do you have any content updates or site changes that you would like to see made to your website? We will work with you to identify your priority updates.  If there is anything you need, just let us know.

Web Content

Content optimized for users with modern SEO practices is a major factor in search ranking. New and updated content not only gives site visitors a reason to keep checking in, search engines recognize this and boost rankings over competition with otherwise similar ranking factors.

Cross Browser Website Testing

We manually review your site using various popular browsers and devices. We not only look for functionality and display issues that will prevent your visitors from using your site, we also look for issues that may affect the impression your website leaves on visitors.

Server Software & Security Updates

Security software and proper server configuration is required to protect your website from attack. Malicious bots crawl the internet searching for sites with vulnerabilities that can be exploited and used to attack other sites, send spam, display advertising, or other malicious content.

A hacked site is best avoided as it can be costly to fix, may tarnish your company image and will result in the removal of your website from search engine search results.
