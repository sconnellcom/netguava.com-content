How It Works

Every Vertex Luthier cello starts with your vision. Our designers work closely with you through every step — from the first sketch to the final performance.

Tell Us Your Theme

Share your concept — dragons, cosmic landscapes, wildlife, fantasy, or anything else you dream up. No idea is too bold.

3D Sculpting

Our artists sculpt a detailed 3D model of your design in digital space, refining it until every detail is perfect.

Review & Approve

You preview digital renders and work with us to fine-tune the design before a single layer is printed.

Print & Integrate

We 3D print the sculpt and engineer it directly into your electric cello body for a seamless, one-of-a-kind instrument.

Finish & Tune

Your cello receives a professional finish and setup so it's ready to perform the moment it arrives in your hands.
